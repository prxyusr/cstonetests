Lead Paint Inspection & Local Law 1 Compliance

EPA Certified XRF Testing & Risk Assessments

Under NYC Local Law 1, landlords and property managers must ensure apartments in multiple dwellings where a child under six resides are free of lead-based paint hazards. Our certified inspectors use advanced X-ray fluorescence (XRF) analyzers to instantly and accurately detect lead paint layers without destructive sampling.

XRF Testing

Non-destructive, instant quantitative analysis of painted surfaces across units.

Risk Assessments

Detailed dust wipe sampling, soil testing, and visual degradation reports.

Violation Clearances

Official documentation and filing support to dismiss HPD or DHCR lead orders.

Mold Assessment & Indoor Air Quality

Air & Surface Sampling

Thermal Imaging

Remediation Protocols
